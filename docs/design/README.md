# Waymark: the look of Switchback

`waymark-mockups.html` holds the mock-ups the owner approved (open it in a browser; it is one self-contained
page). The apps are built to it. Its `:root` block is the source of the colours, type and shapes.

What was decided:

- **Soft cards over the map**, spruce green, cool stone surfaces. **Sign yellow means you**: your position on
  the map and on the ridge, the next thing ahead, and the off-trail card. Nothing else is yellow.
- **Blaze marks** show difficulty by count (one easy, two moderate, three hard), in the colour the park paints
  that trail where we know it, green otherwise. Each trail card carries the trail's own shape.
- **Bottom row:** four plain words of equal weight, Explore, Hike, Saved, Log, with a yellow blaze beside the
  current one. Hike is where a hike is recorded.
- **List:** a trail with a short loop shows two lines, each with its own length and climb; tapping one opens the
  trail set to it. (May be removed later.)
- **Trail page:** distance, climb and time are the largest things after the name. One compact card holds the
  elevation ridge, with the main things on the way pinned to it, and a single daylight line under it. Start hike
  is a solid green button with the switchback mark.
- **While walking:** the map, and one card with the ridge (you on it in yellow, the next thing named), two big
  numbers (miles left, back by) and one quiet line (time on the trail, walked, climbed). The bottom row steps
  away during a hike. Pulled up: moving time, pace, climb left, sunset, light to spare, height now, coordinates
  with Share and GPS accuracy, then Pause, Mark a spot and Finish (Finish asks once).
- **At the end** of the trail, or back at the start of a loop, the app asks once whether to finish, on screen and
  as a notification. Finish, Keep going, or ignore it.
- **Off the trail:** the card turns yellow and says how far and which way.
- **Lock screen** (later): an iPhone Live Activity and a pinned Android notification with miles left, back by,
  the next thing and the ridge.
